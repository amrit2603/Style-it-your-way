## Overview

Portrait garment photography leads the storefront. White surfaces, restrained gold actions and compact typography follow the supplied Panna reference with VYUMI branding. The merchant studio uses the same controls in a denser working layout.

## Colors

Gold identifies primary actions. Forest supports the shopping assistant. Paper and warm canvas separate content from work surfaces; ink and muted text establish reading order. Danger and focus have distinct functional roles.

## Typography

Self-hosted Urbanist is the interface face. The centered VYUMI wordmark uses Georgia with generous letter spacing. Keep product metadata smaller than page headings and keep upload guidance readable beside controls.

## Layout

Desktop collection pages show four portrait columns; mobile shows two. Responsive changes occur at 700, 1000 and 1600 pixels. The merchant editor follows Details, Images, Generate, then Review & publish. The product-specific styling page places the selected product beside photo guidance and generation controls. On mobile the product becomes a compact summary above the form.

Collection tabs separate Everyone, Women, Men and Unisex. A labelled garment-type selector filters the portrait grid independently. The styling page locks the selected product. Four labelled view buttons switch between independent photo/result states. Personal previews keep original and result adjacent with direct download. Consent and one photo upload start generation automatically; optional user-supplied measurements stay collapsed until needed.

## Elevation & Depth

Product cards remain flat. Reserve shadows for the floating assistant launcher and overlaid drawer so their relationship to the page stays clear.

## Shapes

Controls use small corners and thin borders. Product photographs remain rectangular; the assistant launcher is circular.

## Components

Primary buttons use gold with white text and darken on hover. Keyboard focus has a visible green outline. Disabled and in-progress actions retain explanatory text. Gallery labels sit away from image watermarks. Styling uses normal page navigation with a back-to-product link, visible keyboard focus and no focus trap. PDF cropping supports pointer and numeric controls. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential transitions.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do preserve generous portrait image space and compact metadata. Do keep draft, generated, approved and published states explicit. Do label demo results visibly. Don't place the floating launcher over important product actions or cover image labels with other badges.
