# VYUMI Style It Your Way
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-selected local Next.js + FastAPI prototype, SQLite and private local files.

Platform expansion is staged backend → Kaggle notebook → independent admin → independent customer app. The backend adds account/password authentication, server-priced unpaid orders, stock-model/room libraries and a Kaggle adapter while retaining the existing prototype and data. See docs/PLATFORM_BUILD.md and docs/BACKEND.md for checkpoint scope and verification.

## Users
Shopkeepers prepare product catalogs; shoppers browse garments and preview them on their own photo. Initial testing uses consenting adults.

## Product Purpose
Convert garment photos or selected PDF catalog regions into reviewed catalog images, then offer private product-specific visual try-on.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Photo and PDF input; men's, women's and unisex collections; thirteen product categories including shoes, watches and furniture; one selected product per shopper page; draft/review/publish workflow; dedicated styling page with separate front/back/left/right photo uploads, illustrated capture guidance and optional measurements; private one-hour photo lifetime; backend Cloudflare, Gemini or FASHN integration; dedicated FASHN public-demo personal try-on; no checkout or fit guarantees. Consented photo upload starts generation automatically. Shared GPU availability is limited and real-person accuracy is unbenchmarked. User approved the design and delegated implementation without repeated approval prompts.

## Brand Commitments
VYUMI identity. Follow the supplied Panna storefront recording: portrait garment photography, compact white header, four-column desktop catalog, gold actions and a right-side chat drawer. Use original or licensed imagery, not Panna assets.

## Evidence on Hand
Approved design and implementation plan in docs/superpowers; reference observations in docs/reference/DESIGN.md. No licensed garment catalog or consented personal test set has been supplied. Do not fabricate testimonials, inventory, discounts or successful AI trials.

## Product Principles
Preserve garment evidence. Review before publication. Keep personal images private. Clearly distinguish fixtures, generated visuals and real product information.
